About Our School

A place where every child is encouraged to discover their potential

Abhishek Haldar School is a co-educational English-medium school affiliated to CBSE, offering classes from Nursery to Class XII. Our experienced faculty, modern infrastructure and child-centric approach help students excel in academics, sports and co-curricular activities.

🎯 Our Vision

To create confident, responsible and compassionate citizens who are ready for the challenges of tomorrow.

🚀 Our Mission

  • Provide quality education in a safe and joyful environment
  • Encourage curiosity, creativity and critical thinking
  • Instil moral values, discipline and respect for all

📝 Admission Process

  • Collect / download the admission form from the school office.
  • Submit the form with required documents.
  • Interaction / entrance test (Class I onwards).
  • Confirmation of seat and fee deposit.

📂 Documents Required

  • Birth certificate
  • Aadhaar card of student & parents
  • Transfer certificate & previous report card
  • 4 passport-size photographs
